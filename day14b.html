1

Build a Piece of It

~35 minutes · solo or with your partner · pick YOUR station

Not the whole project. One small real piece of it. Enough to know what the full build will take.

🗺️ Story map / narrated tour: open Map Studio. Drop your first 3 pins. Write one pin's story in your person's voice. Try the 1717 Chart mode. Save it (it stays on your machine).
🖼️ Poster / exhibit board / newspaper: grab scrap paper and sketch the whole layout small — every box, every image, every label. This sketch IS your supply list generator.
📖 Flip book / graphic novel / letters: make page one for real. Fold it, draw the first panel, or write the first letter. Feel how long one unit takes, then multiply.
🎭 Performance / shanty: write your first 6 lines and say them to a wall. Then to a partner. Time it. Multiply to 3–5 minutes and see what's missing.
🎬 Slides story: storyboard all 10–12 slides on paper first — title + image idea + one quote each. Then see Max about the no-login build plan.
LOG IT: On THE TEST page: what you built · one thing that worked · one thing that fought you.

Format fought you the whole time? That's the trial run doing its job. Switch to your other format NOW and test that instead — today is the free-switch day.

2

Write THE LIST

~10 minutes · solo · this is the one Max needs

You just built a piece of it. Now you know what the real thing needs. Write it exact, boring, complete:

  1. Physical project? Every item: board size, how many markers, what colors, pictures (name them — Max prints at home, once), glue, string, tape. "Art stuff" buys nothing. "One poster board, four black markers, glue stick" builds a project.
  2. All-digital? Write ALL-DIGITAL big on THE LIST page — and note anything you still need from Max (a printed map? a practice slot?).
  3. Performing? Your list might be: printed script, costume bits, a prop. List them.
LOG IT: THE LIST page is tomorrow's form, pre-written. Copy it over in ink tomorrow and you're done in two minutes.

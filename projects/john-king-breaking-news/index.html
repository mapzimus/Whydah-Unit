The report

John King, then aged between eight and eleven, was a passenger on the Bonetta. According to Abijah Savage, the Bonetta's commander, the pirate looted the ship for 15 days during which time King demanded to join Bellamy's crew [scribbled-out word] from being forced [scribbled-out word] or compelled to join. Savage wrote a report: "He declaird he would kill him self if he was a restraid or [scribbled-out word] threatend his mother."

Abijah Savage, commander of the Bonetta, testified after Bellamy released the ship in November 1716: King was “so far from being forced or compelled” that he “declared he would kill himself if he was restrained, and even threatened his mother.” See People and Methods.
The Whydah Gally — a slave ship turned pirate ship, wrecked off Cape Cod in 1717. Claims are tiered Solid / Contested / Mythologized.

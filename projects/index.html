Student projects

The final projects from the Navigating Piracy Showcase at Collins Middle School — the digital ones, story maps built in Map Studio, and the physical ones, posters and writing pieces photographed and transcribed. All of it committed here so it outlasts the classroom.

Students are credited by first name only — as written on the work, or as provided by the class facilitator. Handwritten pieces are transcribed with the student's wording, spelling, and punctuation preserved.

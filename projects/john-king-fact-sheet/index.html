John King, the youngest known pirate

By Kelly and Jasmely. Writing on John King. They also wrote The Story of Albus Sharktooth.

The notes

Handwritten text is transcribed with the students' wording, spelling, and punctuation preserved; letter case is normalized for legibility.

Note 1

John King was a 13 years boy that turn into a pirate when he was 13. John King was an 18-centrey pirate. He joined the crew of Samuel Bellamy while still a juvenile and may have been the youngest knowl pirate record

Note 2

  • John was originally a passenger on the ship Bonetta with his mother when Bellamys pirates capture it in 1716 ("when" is inserted above the line with a caret in the original)
  • He sailed abord the famous pirate ship Whyday Gally Bellamys flagship
King boarded the Bonetta with his mother in November 1716 and demanded to join Bellamy’s crew. Age at death: 8–11. See People, and Erick’s writing and John King: The Boy Pirate.
The Whydah Gally — a slave ship turned pirate ship, wrecked off Cape Cod in 1717. Claims are tiered Solid / Contested / Mythologized.
